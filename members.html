The roster

The member directory, by organization.

The seat belongs to the organization. Development roles turn over; membership should not. When a member moves on, the organization stays on this roster and their successor is welcome to step into the seat. New in your role? Email us and we will update the listing and add you to the group.

Listings come from the group roster. Contact information appears only with each member's explicit OK: add your email via the roster sheet or the address below, and if you would rather not be listed at all, one email removes you, no questions asked.
